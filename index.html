    How to survive the night

    • Find the fuses hidden around the school and bring them to the fuse box in the Boiler Room.
    • When the power comes back on, the front doors unlock. Everyone gets out — or nobody does.
    • The Hall Monitor hears running and sees light. Walk. Crouch. Kill your flashlight when it's close.
    • Hide in lockers and closets. If it stops right outside, hold your breath.
    • Caught? Crawl. A friend can revive you before it comes back.
